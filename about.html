About Samarth Hospital

A multi-speciality hospital serving Pipariya and surrounding areas.

Hospital Overview

Samarth Hospital is a 25-bed multi-speciality hospital located at Pushp Vatika, Sandiya Road, Maharana Pratap Ward in Pipariya, Madhya Pradesh (PIN 461775). The hospital provides healthcare services across General Medicine, Orthopaedic Surgery, and Dental care under the direction of Dr. Tanay Surjan.

We are committed to delivering safe and accessible patient care, following ethical healthcare practices, and maintaining high standards in bio-medical waste segregation and environmental protection.
