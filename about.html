About Samarth Hospital

A 25-bed multi-speciality hospital in Pipariya, Madhya Pradesh.

Hospital Overview

Samarth Hospital is a 25-bed multi-speciality hospital located at Maharana Pratap Ward, Sandiya Road, Pipariya, Madhya Pradesh (PIN 461775). Departments: General Medicine, Orthopaedic Surgery and Dental. Owner: Dr. Tanay Surjan.

Our tagline, motto, vision and mission

Tagline

Saving lives with caring hands

Motto

To provide advanced and quality healthcare to rural and semi-urban region at affordable price

Vision

Quality healthcare for all at affordable costs.

सभी के लिए सस्ती और गुणवत्तापूर्ण स्वास्थ्य सेवा।

Mission

To serve rural communities with compassion and modern care.
