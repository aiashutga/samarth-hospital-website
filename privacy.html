Contact Form & Your Information

When you send a message through the contact form on this website, your name, phone number, and message are transmitted through a third-party form service (Web3Forms) and delivered to the hospital by email.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell, share, or use your information for marketing purposes.

Please do not share sensitive medical information, test results, or personal health details through the contact form. For medical matters, please call the hospital directly or visit in person.
