Environment Protection & Bio-Medical Waste Management

Samarth Hospital sends its bio-medical waste to a common bio-medical waste treatment facility (CBWTF) for treatment and disposal, and records the quantities month by month in four colour categories. Proper handling of this waste helps protect patients, staff, waste handlers and the wider community.

Period Calendar year 2025
Approx. total 1,495.81 kg*
Treatment 70% incineration, 30% autoclaving*
Handed to CBWTF, Sehore
