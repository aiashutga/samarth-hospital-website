Departments

Departments available at Samarth Hospital, Pipariya.

Descriptions are general information, not medical advice.

General Medicine

Care for adults with common medical problems such as fever, infections, diabetes and high blood pressure. Patients who need more tests or specialist care are advised on the next steps.

Consultant details: please call the hospital.

Orthopaedic Surgery

Care for problems of the bones, joints, muscles and ligaments, such as fractures, injuries and joint or back pain. The department advises on treatment, including surgery where needed.
